01 — WHO WE ARE

Every space has a story. We help shape its details.

Door Studio brings together the details that make a house feel considered, complete and truly yours.

We believe doors and hardware are more than functional elements. They are part of the character of a space — the handle you reach for every day, the finish that complements your interiors, and the small details that make a lasting impression.

Our aim is to make discovering and choosing those details a more inspiring, simple and thoughtful experience.
